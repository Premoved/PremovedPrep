Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026

Controller

Armand Veress, acting as an individual. contact@premoved.com

What is processed

  • Account — username, email address, password hash, registration date, and the version of the terms you accepted.
  • Content — your collections, repertoires, saved PGN games and interface preferences.
  • Technical — IP address and browser user agent at registration, sign-in and password reset, and in server logs.

Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash and cannot be read back by anyone.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • Account and content, to provide the service you signed up for — performance of a contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Technical data, to prevent automated abuse and keep the service running — legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Email, to confirm your account, reset your password and tell you about changes affecting your account — performance of a contract. There is no marketing email.

Analytics and cookies

There is no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. Your browser stores only your session token and your interface preferences, both of which are needed for the site to work.

Processors

Cloudflare (delivery and protection of the site), PidginHost (the server), and Resend (account email). Where data leaves the European Union it travels under the standard contractual clauses those providers publish.

Retention

Account data is kept until you delete your account. An account that is never confirmed by email is deleted automatically. Deleting your account removes your username, email address, password hash, collections, saved games, sessions and agent keys, immediately and irreversibly. If a refund was ever granted, a record of it remains with no link to you — the account reference is removed and only a one-way hash of the email address is kept — so that the refund policy cannot be avoided by closing an account.

Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Erasure is also available immediately in Settings. Write to contact@premoved.com. You may also complain to the Romanian supervisory authority ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro) or to the authority where you live.

Children

The service is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

The date above changes when this policy does. A material change is announced by email.
