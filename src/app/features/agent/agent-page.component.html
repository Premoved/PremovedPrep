Desktop agent

A small application that runs on your own computer and acts as a bridge between this site and your local device: enables you to use your own game archive as the Database, your own chess engine for analysis, and as much storage as your disk has.

Storage

Your Library and Repertoire are kept on this computer as well as in your account.

There is nothing to choose here. Whatever fits in your account's allowance of 5 MB stays in the cloud so you have it anywhere; anything past it lives on your computer disk and appears in your Library and Repertoire while the agent is running. Which entries stay in the cloud is decided in the agent, on its Local backup screen.
